# PAGE SPECIFICATION — THE EIGHT MONEY PAGES

These eight pages carry the revenue. They get the most links, the most photography, the
most video, and all ongoing optimization. Every other page on the site exists to support
them.

---

## THE COMMERCIAL PAGE SKELETON

Every commercial page follows this 12-part structure. It serves SEO, AI extraction, and
conversion at the same time.

| # | Block | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **H1 + direct-answer paragraph** | Answer what the service is, immediately. This is the paragraph AI engines quote. |
| 2 | **Problem recognition** | "Is this happening with your dog?" Reader self-identifies. |
| 3 | **Solution** | How Floppy Ears Ranch handles it. |
| 4 | **Programs** | What can they actually buy. |
| 5 | **Results** | Real evidence — video, before/after, case studies. |
| 6 | **Trainer credibility** | Who does the work, with credentials. |
| 7 | **Facility** | Where it happens. Photos. |
| 8 | **Pricing** | Real numbers from `pricing.md`. |
| 9 | **Process** | What happens next, step by step. |
| 10 | **FAQ** | Answer purchase objections. `FAQPage` schema. |
| 11 | **Geography** | Where clients come from. Internal links to service areas. |
| 12 | **CTA** | One clear action. |

### The direct-answer rule

Under every H1, and under every question-shaped H2, write a **2–4 sentence factual
answer** before any elaboration. Plain language, specific numbers, no marketing adjectives.

- ✅ "A 3-week Board & Train at Floppy Ears Ranch costs $2,900. Your dog lives at our
  5-acre Corona facility and trains daily with Chad and Kyla Gabriel. The price includes
  lifetime support, free refresher lessons, and a transfer session where we teach you to
  handle everything your dog learned."
- ❌ "Our premier Board & Train experience is second to none, delivering world-class
  results for discerning dog owners throughout Southern California."

The first gets quoted by ChatGPT. The second gets skipped.
