## Board & Train

**Body**
Your dog moves in with us and trains every day. No squeezing sessions into an already full week, no waiting for progress between appointments. Most dogs are here for three weeks. Puppies stay six.

## When it's more than obedience

**Body**
If your dog lunges at every dog on the block, guards the couch, or has put teeth on someone, you already know this isn't a manners problem. You've probably also been told by somebody that your dog is a lost cause.

We don't start there. We start with an evaluation, because aggression and reactivity have different causes, different triggers, and different answers. A dog who's terrified needs something different than a dog who's never been told no.

We'll tell you honestly what we think we can do — and if we don't think we're the right fit, we'll say that too.

**Two cards**

**Reactive Dog Training** → `/reactive-dog-training/`
Barking, lunging, pulling, and losing it at the sight of a trigger.

**Aggressive Dog Training** → `/aggressive-dog-training/`

**H2**
## Common questions

**Where are you located?**
Floppy Ears Ranch is at 21761 Knabe Rd, Corona, CA 92883, on five acres in the Temescal Valley area of Corona. We serve Riverside County, the Inland Empire, Orange County, and the surrounding parts of Southern California.

**How much does dog training cost?**
Board & Train programs run $2,900 to $4,500 depending on the program and your dog's needs. Private lessons are $200 per session, or $900 for five. Boarding starts at $80 per night and daycare is $40 per day. The first phone consultation is free.

**What training methods do you use?**
We build on relationship and marker training first, then add clear corrections so a dog understands both sides of the conversation. We use an e-collar in the Off-Leash Reliability Program, and the equipment is included in the price. We'll explain exactly what we use and why before you commit to anything.

**Do you work with aggressive dogs?**
Yes. We run a three-week Behavior Rehabilitation Program for aggression, bite histories, and severe reactivity. Every dog is evaluated individually, because acceptance depends on the specific case.

**How long does training take?**
Board & Train is three weeks for dogs over six months and six weeks for puppies. Private lesson clients usually see meaningful change in five to twelve weeks depending on how consistently the work happens at home.

**Do you serve Orange County?**
Yes, for Board & Train and behavior work. We're 25 to 40 minutes from Yorba Linda, Anaheim Hills, Brea, and Fullerton, and 40 to 55 from Irvine and Newport. We don't offer daycare or routine boarding at that distance — it's too far to drive daily.

**What's the first step?**
A free 30-minute phone consultation. Tell us what's going on with your dog and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.
