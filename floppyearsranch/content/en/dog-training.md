## SECTION 5 — TRAINING METHOD

**H2**
## What training method do we use?

We're a balanced training facility. That means we start with relationship and marker training to teach a dog what to do, and we add clear corrections so the dog also understands what not to do. We use an e-collar in the Off-Leash Reliability Program, where the equipment is included in the price, and sometimes in other programs depending on the dog.

**Engagement first.** Before any command, your dog learns that paying attention to a person is worth doing. Skip this and everything downstream is a fight.

**Marker training.** A clear signal that means *that was right*. It's how we tell a dog exactly which choice earned the reward instead of leaving them to guess.

**Then the other half of the conversation.** A dog who only ever hears yes has no way to sort a good choice from a bad one. We teach a consistent no, fairly, at a level the dog can actually work under.

We say this out loud because you deserve to know before you book, not halfway through. Some trainers in this area are purely positive-reinforcement, and that's a legitimate approach — if that's what you want, we'd rather you find it than be surprised by us. You'll never discover mid-program that we're using a tool you weren't expecting.

## Dog training FAQ

**How much does dog training cost in Corona, CA?**
Board & Train programs at Floppy Ears Ranch run $2,900 to $4,500 depending on the program. Private lessons are $200 per session or $900 for five. A virtual consultation is $139. The first 30-minute phone consultation is free, and the in-person facility evaluation is $50, credited in full toward any program you book.

**How long does dog training take?**
Board & Train is three weeks for dogs six months and older, and six weeks for puppies six months and under. Private lesson clients usually see meaningful change in five to twelve weeks, depending entirely on how consistently the work happens at home between sessions.

**What's the difference between Board & Train and private lessons?**
In Board & Train your dog lives here and trains daily with us, then we hand the training to you in a transfer session at the end. In private lessons you do the training and we coach you through it, one session at a time. Board & Train is faster and costs more. Private lessons cost less and require your time every week.

**Do you train dogs of any age?**
Yes. Puppies six months and under go into the six-week Puppy Board & Train. Dogs six months and older go into the three-week program or the Behavior Rehabilitation Program depending on what's happening. Older dogs learn fine — the "old dog, new tricks" thing isn't true in any practical sense.

**Do you use e-collars?**
Yes, in the Off-Leash Reliability Program where the equipment is included in the $4,500, and sometimes in other programs depending on the dog. We tell you exactly what we plan to use and why before you book, and we teach you to use it properly before your dog goes home.

**Do you work with aggressive dogs?**
Yes. The three-week Behavior Rehabilitation Program is $3,500 and exists for aggression, bite histories, and severe reactivity. Every dog is evaluated individually, because acceptance depends on the specific case and not every dog is a candidate.

**Where are you located?**
Floppy Ears Ranch is at 21761 Knabe Rd, Corona, CA 92883, on five acres in the Temescal Valley area of Corona, in Riverside County.

**What's the first step?**
A free 30-minute phone or video consultation. Tell us what's going on and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help and which program fits. If you want to come out, the in-person facility evaluation is $50 and comes off whatever you book.
