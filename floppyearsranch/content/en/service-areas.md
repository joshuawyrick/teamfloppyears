## SECTION 2 — THE HONEST VERSION

**H2**
## How far we actually go

Most dog training websites list forty cities at the bottom of every page. It's a keyword move and everyone in the industry knows it — but it tells you nothing, because a facility in Corona cannot realistically run daycare for a dog in Irvine no matter how many times it types the word.

The thing that changes with distance isn't quality. It's frequency. Daycare only works if you can swing by twice in a day without reorganizing your life. Boarding works if the drive is one you'd make for a long weekend. Board & Train works from almost anywhere, because your dog lives here for three to six weeks and you make exactly two trips: drop-off, and the transfer session where we hand the training back to you.

That's the whole logic. Everything below follows from it.

## SECTION 6 — WHY THIS MATTERS TO YOU

**H2**
## Why we bother drawing the line

Because the alternative wastes your time. If you're in Costa Mesa and you call about daycare, the useful answer is "no, and here's why" — not a quote you'll cancel in a month.

Distance also changes what we recommend. A Corona client who can come back every other week for a tune-up gets a different plan than a Newport client realistically making one more trip out. We build around that from the start instead of pretending geography isn't a factor.
