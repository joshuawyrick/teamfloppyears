## SECTION 2 — WHAT IS BOARD & TRAIN?

**H2**
## What is Board & Train?

Board & Train means your dog moves in with us and trains daily instead of once or twice a week. It removes the two things that stall most dog training: not enough repetitions, and too much time between them.

A dog in a weekly lesson program gets maybe an hour of structured work every seven days, then goes home and practices whatever they were already doing. A dog here works multiple times a day, every day, in an environment we control — and then gets to practice around real distractions before they go home.

That's the honest version of why it costs what it costs. You're not paying for a magic technique. You're paying for concentrated time, from people who do this all day.

**What Board & Train is not:** it isn't a factory reset. Your dog doesn't come back a different animal, and they don't stay trained on their own. The training holds because we hand it to you properly at the end and stay available afterward. Any program that skips that part is selling you three weeks of rented behavior.

## SECTION 3 — IS THIS RIGHT FOR YOUR DOG?

**H2**
## Is Board & Train right for your dog?

### H3 — Board & Train is usually the right call when

- You've tried training on your own and hit a wall
- Your schedule genuinely won't support consistent daily practice for the next two months
- The problem is serious enough that waiting makes it worse — reactivity, aggression, bolting out doors
- You have a puppy and you'd rather build the foundation right than fix it later
- You want off-leash reliability, which takes more proofing than most owners can produce at home
- Multiple people in the house are handling the dog differently and nothing is sticking

### H3 — Private lessons are probably the better fit when

- You want to learn to train the dog yourself, and you've got the time
- The issue is narrow and specific — one command, one situation
- Your dog is extremely fearful and needs to build trust slowly before living somewhere new
- Budget matters more than speed

We'll tell you which one we think you need. If that's private lessons, we'll say so — a client who books the wrong program is a bad outcome for both of us.

## SECTION 6 — WHAT YOUR DOG LEARNS

**H2**
## What your dog learns

Every program is built on the same foundation, then shaped to your dog.

### H3 — The foundation
**Engagement.** Before anything else, your dog learns that paying attention to a person is worth doing. Everything downstream depends on it.

**Marker training.** A clear signal that means *that was right.* It's how we tell a dog exactly which choice earned the reward, instead of leaving them to guess.

**Clear communication.** Dogs aren't confused by rules. They're confused by rules that change depending on who's in the room. We teach a consistent yes and a consistent no, and your dog stops having to guess.

### H3 — The obedience
Sit · Down · Stay · Place · Heel · Come when called · Leave it · Off · Wait at doorways

### H3 — The real-world part
Commands in a quiet yard are easy. We proof the work — around other dogs, around people, around noise and movement and the things your dog actually cares about. A recall that only works in your kitchen isn't a recall.

### H3 — The living-with-you part
Loose-leash walking · Settling on a place cot · Crate comfort · Impulse control · Polite greetings · Handling for grooming and the vet · Potty routine

Most facilities won't tell you this. Here's ours.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Morning** | Out first thing, potty, and a check on how everyone slept. Breakfast, then medications for the dogs who need them. |

## SECTION 12 — WHEN YOUR DOG COMES HOME

**H2**
## What happens when your dog comes home

This is the part that decides whether the money was worth it.

### H3 — The transfer session
Your dog has learned a new way of communicating. You haven't. So before your dog goes home, we spend real time putting the leash in your hand and coaching you through every command — timing, body language, how to hold a boundary without getting loud about it.

You'll practice until it works for you, not until we've demonstrated it. There's a difference, and it's the difference between training that lasts and training that evaporates in a week.

We'll also be straight with you about what will slip. Some things will. Knowing which ones and what to do is most of the battle.

### H3 — Free refresher lessons
Come back. Bring the dog. No charge.

Life happens — you get busy, standards drift, something new starts up. A refresher session catches it before it becomes the original problem again.

### H3 — Lifetime support
Text us in six months. Text us in three years. Ask a question, send a video, tell us something's off.

There's no expiration on this and no upsell attached. We'd rather answer a text than watch a dog we trained end up back where they started.

## SECTION 16 — FAQ

**H2**
## Board & Train FAQ

**How much does Board & Train cost in Corona, CA?**
Our Board & Train programs cost $2,900 for a three-week adult program, $3,200 for a six-week puppy program, $3,500 for the three-week Behavior Rehabilitation Program, and $4,500 for the Off-Leash Reliability Program. Every price includes the transfer session, free refresher lessons, lifetime support, and all training equipment.

**How long does Board & Train take?**
Three weeks for dogs six months and older. Six weeks for puppies six months and under, because puppies learn better with repetition spread over more time. The Off-Leash Reliability Program length depends on where your dog starts.

**Does Board & Train actually work?**
Yes, when the handoff is done properly. Your dog will learn what we teach — that part is straightforward. Whether it lasts depends on the transfer session and what happens at home afterward, which is why we spend real time coaching you and keep supporting you for free after your dog leaves. A program that ends the moment you pick up your dog is where Board & Train gets its bad reputation.

**Will my dog still listen to me afterward?**
That's exactly what the transfer session is for. Your dog learns a new way of communicating, and you need to learn it too. We put the leash in your hand and coach you through every command until it works for you, not just for us. Then you get free refresher lessons for as long as you need them.

**Can an aggressive dog do Board & Train?**
Often, yes — that's what the three-week Behavior Rehabilitation Program is for. But we evaluate every dog individually before accepting the case, because aggression has different causes and not every dog is a candidate. If we don't think we can help yours safely, we'll say so.

**Is my puppy too young for Board & Train?**
Puppies six months and under go into the six-week Puppy Board & Train. Younger puppies can start once they've had enough of their vaccination series to be safe around other dogs — we'll walk through the timing on the free consultation.

**Will my dog think I abandoned them?**
No. Dogs live in the present far more than we do, and the ones here are busy, engaged, and getting a lot of attention. Most dogs settle within the first couple of days. You'll see it yourself in the daily photos — that's part of why we send them.

**Can I visit my dog during the program?**
We'll talk about it on the consultation. For most dogs mid-program visits reset progress and make the adjustment harder rather than easier. Instead you get a photo every day, video every three days, and a call partway through, plus you can text us whenever you want.

**Do you use e-collars?**
We use one in the Off-Leash Reliability Program, where the equipment is included in the price, and sometimes in other programs depending on the dog. We'll tell you exactly what we plan to use and why before you book anything, and we teach you to use it properly before your dog goes home. You'll never find out mid-program that we're using a tool you weren't expecting.

**What vaccinations does my dog need?**
Current rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella. Canine influenza is recommended. Bring records at drop-off.

**What should I pack?**
Your dog's food for the length of the stay, any medications with instructions, and vaccination records. We provide everything else — leashes, collars, bedding, bowls, training equipment. Leave the toys and blankets at home.
