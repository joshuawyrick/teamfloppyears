## SECTION 4 — BEHAVIOR WORK

**H2**
## Aggression and reactivity cases from Riverside

More of our behavior calls come from Riverside than from anywhere else, and there's a straightforward reason: it's a big city with a lot of dogs, a large shelter and rescue pipeline, and a steady supply of adult dogs adopted into homes with no reliable account of their first two years.

If that's your dog, you already know the shape of it. Maybe it was fine for six weeks and then wasn't. Maybe it's fine with you and impossible with anyone else. Maybe somebody has already told you it's a lost cause, or suggested a breed explanation you didn't buy.

We don't start there. We start with an evaluation, because reactivity and aggression have different causes and the cause changes the plan. A dog that's frightened and cornering itself needs something different from a dog that has learned it can move the world by escalating. Sorting out which one you have is most of the work.

**The Behavior Rehabilitation Program is three weeks, $3,500.** Every dog is assessed individually before we accept the case. We won't guarantee an outcome — on aggression, nobody honest will — and if we don't think we can help your dog safely, we'll tell you that instead of taking a deposit.

Common cases from this side of the county:

- Reactivity that built up on a leash, on a sidewalk, over months of repetition
- Dogs that have gotten loose and had a bad encounter, then never recovered from it
- Resource guarding — food, furniture, a person, a doorway
- Rescue dogs with an unknown history and a first bite that came out of nowhere
- Fear-based aggression toward men, toward strangers, or toward the vet
- Two dogs in one house that have started fighting

## SECTION 5 — BOARD & TRAIN

**H2**
## Board & Train, and why the distance helps

There's an argument for training your dog somewhere other than the neighborhood the problem lives in.

A dog that has spent two years rehearsing a routine on the same block — same corner, same fence, same dog behind it — has built that behavior into the environment. Moving the dog out of it for three weeks breaks the pattern before we start rebuilding. Then we proof the new behavior deliberately, around real distractions, so it survives the trip home.

Three weeks for adult dogs at $2,900. Six weeks for puppies at $3,200. Daily training, a photo every day, video every third day, a call partway through, and the transfer session at the end where we put the leash in your hand and coach you until the handling works for you rather than for us.

It isn't a factory reset. Your dog doesn't come back a different animal. It holds because of the handoff and because we stay available afterward — free refresher lessons and lifetime support, with no expiration and no upsell attached.

## SECTION 6 — OFF-LEASH AND EVERYDAY OBEDIENCE

**H2**
## Mount Rubidoux, Sycamore Canyon, and the rest of it

Riverside is a good dog town if your dog is manageable. Mount Rubidoux on a Saturday morning, the trails at Sycamore Canyon and Box Springs, the citrus-lined parkway along Victoria Avenue, the river trail. All of it is fine with a dog that walks on a loose leash and comes when called. All of it is miserable with a dog that doesn't.

That's the practical case for obedience, and it's usually more motivating than the abstract version. Loose-leash walking, a recall that works while the dog is already moving, place and settle, door manners, leave it, and impulse control around the things your dog actually cares about.

For owners who want genuine off-leash freedom, the Off-Leash Reliability Program is $4,500 with the e-collar and all equipment included, and we teach you to use it properly before your dog goes home. We're upfront about that tool because discovering it mid-program is how people end up angry. Worth saying plainly: off-leash reliability is a training standard, not a legal exemption. Riverside has leash requirements and they apply to your very well-trained dog too.

## Riverside dog training FAQ

**Do you offer dog training in Riverside, CA?**
Yes. Our facility is in Corona, 15 to 30 minutes from most of Riverside depending on which part of the city you're in, and every program is available — Board & Train, private lessons, behavior rehabilitation, off-leash work, and boarding.

**How much does dog training cost in Riverside?**
Board & Train is $2,900 for three weeks, $3,200 for the six-week puppy program, $3,500 for the three-week Behavior Rehabilitation Program, and $4,500 for the Off-Leash Reliability Program. Private lessons are $200 a session or $900 for five. The first thirty-minute phone consultation is free and the $50 in-person evaluation is credited toward whatever you book.

**Do you work with aggressive dogs from Riverside?**
Yes — that's what the three-week, $3,500 Behavior Rehabilitation Program is for, and Riverside sends us more behavior cases than any other city. Every dog is evaluated individually before we accept the case, because acceptance depends on the specific dog and what's driving the behavior. We don't guarantee outcomes on aggression, and we'll tell you if we don't think we're the right fit.

**We adopted a rescue and its history is a blank. Where do we start?**
With the free phone consultation, then the $50 in-person evaluation. Unknown history is common and it isn't a barrier — we're reading the dog in front of us, not the paperwork. What we're sorting out is whether the behavior is fear, frustration, guarding, or learned escalation, because each one gets a different plan.

**Is it better to train my dog at home or send it away?**
Depends on the problem and your calendar. Narrow issues and owners with real time to practice do well with private lessons at $200 a session. Long-standing patterns often do better away from the environment they were built in — a dog that has rehearsed the same reaction on the same block for two years benefits from three weeks somewhere else before the rebuild starts.

**Do you offer dog daycare in Riverside?**
Daycare happens at our Corona facility, which is practical if you're in La Sierra, Arlanza, or west Riverside — about fifteen minutes. From the east side of the city it's an hour of driving a day and we'd rather say so than sell you a package you'll stop using. Boarding, on the other hand, works from anywhere in Riverside.

**Can you board a dog that's been kicked out of another kennel?**
Sometimes. Behavior and reactive boarding is $100 a night and requires an assessment first. We take dogs that other facilities decline, but that's not a blanket yes — it depends entirely on what the dog does and whether we can house it safely.

**How far in advance should I book?**
Sooner than feels necessary, especially for summer and the holidays. Board & Train dates fill and a deposit is what holds them — $500 for the standard and puppy programs, $750 for Behavior Rehabilitation and Off-Leash.
