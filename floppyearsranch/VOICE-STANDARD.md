# VOICE STANDARD — approved by Josh, Aug 7 2026

Reference implementations: `content/en/board-and-train.md` and `content/en/home.md`.
READ BOTH BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING. Match them exactly.

## The voice
Plainspoken, confident, occasionally blunt. Never salesy. Writes like a professional who
talks like a real person — not a corporation, not an unedited transcript.

## What makes it work
Telling the truth about limitations. That is the primary conversion tool in this niche,
because every competitor writes "premier" and "world-class" and nobody believes them.

Examples from the approved packs:
- "It isn't a factory reset. Your dog doesn't come back a different animal."
- "Any program that skips that part is selling you three weeks of rented behavior."
- "If that's private lessons, we'll say so — a client who books the wrong program is a
   bad outcome for both of us."
- "A facility that runs dogs all day is producing tired dogs, not trained ones."
- "You'll never find out mid-program that we're using a tool you weren't expecting."

## Rules
- Short sentences. Vary the rhythm. Fragments are fine when they land.
- Second person. "Your dog," not "the client's dog."
- Name the reader's actual situation before offering the solution.
- Concede things. "Some things will slip." "We'll tell you if we're not the right fit."
- Specific numbers over adjectives. "$2,900 for three weeks" beats "affordable."
- NEVER: premier, world-class, cutting-edge, passionate, dedicated, unparalleled,
  state-of-the-art, top-rated, #1, best-in-class, "we pride ourselves."
- NO emoji. NO exclamation points except in genuine quotes.
- Direct-answer paragraph under every H1 and every question-shaped H2: 2-4 sentences,
  factual, specific, quotable by an AI engine.

## Hard constraints
- Every price from `pricing.md`. Never invent one.
- Every fact from `business-facts.md`. If it isn't there, don't write it.
- `[PHONE]` and `[HOURS]` are config tokens. Never guess values.
- NO reviews, star ratings, review counts, awards, or "#1" claims anywhere.
- NO certifications beyond: IACP professional member (Kyla), AKC CGC Evaluator (Kyla).
- NO invented case studies, dog names, or client cities. Placeholders + a ⚠️ note only.
- NO guaranteed outcomes, especially on aggression.
- Mark anything unconfirmed as `⚠️ TODO — [what's needed]` inline.

## File format
Match the approved packs exactly: META block, then numbered SECTION blocks with headings,
finished copy, CTAs, image notes with real alt text, and a BUILD NOTES block at the end.
